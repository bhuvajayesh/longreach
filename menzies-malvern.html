Menzies Malvern

Set on an 8,000sqm site at the corner of Glenferrie Road and High Street Malvern, we developed the Menzies apartments and adjoining medical centre. With interiors* by Carr Design the Menzies has established itself as one of Melbourne's pre-eminent retirement villages.

  • 124 Apartments
  • Joint venture partner: PwC
  • Builder: Multiplex
  • Funding: Commonwealth Bank
  • Management rights: Sold to Lendlease Communities
  • Address: 1306 High Street, Malvern
* While the Menzies' exterior architecture bears some resemblance to the penitentiary in The Shawshank Redemption, we have been assured that any similarity is entirely coincidental, and no design copyright was infringed.
