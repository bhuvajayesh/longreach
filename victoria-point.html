Victoria Point

After acquiring the development rights to the South West Remainder of Stadium Precinct in Melbourne Docklands from Baulderstone Hornibrook, we substantially altered local planning controls and developed Victoria Point:

  • 42-levels (128m)
  • 447 residential apartments
  • 115 serviced apartments
  • Bendigo Bank Melbourne HQ
  • Concourse retail
  • Builder: Multiplex
  • Architect: dKO & Buchan
  • Primary lender: ABN AMRO
  • Address: 100-120 Harbour Esplanade & 744-768 Bourke Street
